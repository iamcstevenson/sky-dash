# Changelog

## November 2026

- **Videos fixes:** opens on Posts only; clicking a #tag word on Everyone no longer fails ("Invalid request"); pages no longer come up empty with **Keep looking** when hidden posts (adult, blocked, muted) fill a page; swear words kept out of the cloud; the cloud stays put while a word is picked.
- **Videos tab reworked:** opens on the newest videos from people you follow (or **Everyone** on Bluesky, using Bluesky's video-only search), with a cloud of up to 20 words to filter by. Adult-labeled posts are left out of Everyone.
- **Following opens on Posts only.**
- **Video links play in place:** YouTube, Dailymotion, Vimeo, Streamable, Twitch clips and TikTok links show as a full-width preview on every tab.
- **Foldable lists:** the keyword list on Keywords and the recent/keyword links on Videos fold away to bring the posts up.
- **Videos tab:** search all of Bluesky for video of sporting events (or anything else), newest or most popular, with recent searches and your keywords one click away. **Videos only** on the Keywords tab does the same for your saved keywords.
- **Group editor lists every saved keyword** to tick, with a search box and a separate box for new ones.
- **Discover panel posts** can be liked, reposted, replied to and quoted in place.
- **Like as…:** pick which account likes a post when several of yours follow its author.
- **Muted words:** a 🔇 box hides posts with chosen words, phrases or #tags from your feeds, kept in step with Bluesky's muted words on all your accounts.
- **Follow pills are buttons:** click an A / B / C follow badge anywhere to unfollow or follow again.
- **Hover cards say whether they follow you** ("Follows you" / "Doesn't follow you").
- **Discover side panel.** "Their posts" opens a panel instead of leaving Discover: profile, Follow / Audition / Not interested, ← → to move through the list, and a snapshot from their last 100 posts (topics, posting mix, rhythm, reception, linked sites, who they talk with) above their recent posts.
- **Videos play in Skydash** instead of opening bsky.app, with a large viewer.
- **Post waits 2 minutes** before going to Bluesky, so typos can be fixed; **Schedule** posts for a time you pick. The server posts them, with no page open.
- **Up to 4 accounts**, added and removed from the dashboard (⚙).
- **Profile cards** show real 14-day activity for anyone, not just people you follow.
- **Self-updating server:** a server install pulls and restarts within 5 minutes of a change to `main`.
